The full service supports individual staff accounts, private manual ID/selfie review, administrator member deletion and direct product-photo uploads. New signups queue transactional welcome emails; delivery requires the owner's authenticated sending account in private hosting settings. No third-party identity service, automated face matching or liveness detection is connected.

This static catalog is a bundled snapshot and does not receive staff catalog changes automatically. Management, account access, identity uploads and order requests require the full Node service and its private hosting setup. Source and the complete client ZIP are on loud-cana-client-handoff.

Members now receive permanent membership numbers and a digital card in the full-service dashboard. Admin and individual staff accounts have private membership/verification/profile notifications with personal unread status. These in-app alerts update while the protected dashboard is open; the static catalog does not expose account information.

Approved members keep their Verified member card badge; completed ID/selfie review panels and setup checklists are hidden from their full-service dashboard. New required reviews restore those steps.

The full application now uses LC- plus six-digit member IDs, with barcodes on digital cards. Both protected workspaces include member-ID search and local camera/image/USB barcode lookup. Earlier card numbers remain lookup aliases. Scan results show the current account status from the authenticated service.

The full service now provides administrator-only processed merchandise reports with saved product selections, SAST dates and completing-staff attribution. Members can view merchandise reward tokens at R10 for every cumulative R1,000 of eligible externally paid merchandise. An administrator confirms actual payment before credit is issued; no payment processor or automatic historical credit is connected. These features require the full Node service and apply to eligible non-cannabis merchandise only.

Administrators can now create an Accessories draft directly from Processed reports using Add product, then save the report selection. Photos and separate publication are managed from Products in the full service.
