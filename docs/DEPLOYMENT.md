# Deploy Loud + Cana

Use the full Node service for persistent order requests and the protected `/admin` and `/staff` workspaces. The application is hosted on Render, and a separate static collection website is published on GitHub Pages. A successful deployment does not establish commercial sales authorization or readiness to accept orders.

## Membership deployment

Members, administrators and staff use the same **`/login`** page with its generic email-or-username field. Members enter their registered email, the administrator enters **`admin`**, and employees enter the individual usernames created through **Admin → Staff accounts**. Server-validated roles route to **`/account`**, **`/admin`** and **`/staff`** respectively. Public pages no longer advertise staff access. `ADMIN_PASSWORD` bootstraps administrator access; employees have separate salted, hashed credentials stored in SQLite. `STAFF_PASSWORD` is a legacy shared-login option and should remain blank when individual accounts are used. See [the exact Render and staff-account setup steps](STAFF_SETUP.md).

Member accounts operate independently of order activation. Account access does not require `LIVE_ORDERS_ENABLED` or catalog approval, but authorised management access is necessary for member management, product management and manual review. Configure `ADMIN_PASSWORD` for the administrator, then create individual employees from the private Staff accounts tab. Staff access stays unavailable until an active employee account has been created, unless the optional legacy shared login is deliberately configured. New identity submissions additionally require a valid **`IDENTITY_UPLOAD_KEY`**; missing or invalid key configuration rejects image submission instead of storing unprotected photographs. An account remains restricted until its required ID/selfie review is completed. Keep order intake disabled while the business requirements above are pending. The member service creates and migrates its tables in the same SQLite database on startup; no member credentials or signing secrets belong in source.

Signup now uses a server-side 18+ date-of-birth check, separate age-check consent, and three random responsible-use questions. `GET /api/members/signup-challenge` creates a browser-bound 15-minute challenge with shuffled choices, at most three answer attempts, and single-use enforcement. New applications also require a government-issued photo ID image and a face photograph through the authenticated secure account flow, with specific upload/review consent. The member remains restricted until manual staff review. Older applications retain their legacy original-ID path, but new submissions use the ID/selfie workflow. A profile name change invalidates age verification and removes pending images. Questions, dates and image uploads are not an automatic identity result; no automated face matching, liveness service or third-party identity provider is connected.

Configure the staff and upload secrets securely in Render, then use **`/staff` → Age Checks**. Staff privately view the submitted ID and selfie, compare the ID photograph, full name and birth date against the selfie and application, and record both required identity-comparison and 18+ attestations through the confirmation step. Do not approve an unseen image or treat a questionnaire as verification. An uploaded photograph does not prove a document is genuine or the person is live. Legacy in-person original-ID reviews are available only for older records. Order submission still requires a currently verified signed-in member plus every business-sales gate. Read [the South African signup guide](SOUTH_AFRICAN_SIGNUP.md) and [encrypted upload instructions](IDENTITY_VERIFICATION.md) before operating the queue. Do not request ID images by email or a contact form.

**Before accepting member registrations, verify the existing service's persistent disk and set `DATABASE_PATH=/var/data/orders.sqlite` with a disk mounted at `/var/data`. The current disk mount cannot be verified through the public API.** An ephemeral database can lose accounts, favourites, sessions, and order records on redeployment. The Blueprint includes a persistent disk, but an existing manually configured Render service must be checked in the owner's dashboard. Keep a tested backup and restore procedure.

After the latest `main` deployment, check `/api/health`, `/api/members/me`, `/membership`, `/signup`, `/login`, and `/account` on the actual HTTPS service. A guest request to `/api/members/me` should return `{ "member": null }`. In an authorised local test environment, verify under-18 and invalid-date rejection, random challenge replacement/expiry/replay limits, pending signup, legacy member onboarding, staff rejection and approval, birth-date purging, name-change invalidation, logout/login, profile changes, cross-device favourites, password changes, and account deletion. Verify a pending or rejected member cannot submit an order even if business-sales flags are enabled. Use synthetic images in local acceptance tests. Never record a real verification without performing the applicable manual comparison, and never approve a production test fixture as a real person. Restart the test service once to confirm account and session persistence. Do not use a real customer's account for acceptance tests.

Passwords use salted scrypt hashes with a 12–128 character input limit. Member sessions expire after 30 days and use opaque tokens, hashed at rest, in HttpOnly SameSite cookies marked Secure in production. A password change replaces the current session and revokes other sessions. Account deletion requires the member's password and removes the member profile, saved favourites, and sessions; independent order records are not cancelled. Staff sessions and member sessions are separate.

Administrators can also delete registered accounts from **Members → Delete member** after typing the member's email and confirming. This admin-only request requires the currently displayed `updatedAt` version and is rejected if the profile has changed. It removes the profile, favourites, all member sessions, linked age-check/identity records and unsent welcome jobs. Independent orders remain and are not cancelled. Minimal administrative deletion audit metadata and historical backups have separate retention; delivered or already-in-flight email cannot be recalled. Staff can read and review members but cannot delete them. Closing an account does not ban the person from signing up again.

New registrations enqueue a transactional welcome email; delivery requires the SMTP or Resend setup below. This feature does not provide email verification, email-based password recovery or staff invitations. Registration, a welcome message and passing questions do not prove email ownership, identity or age; only an actually performed staff review using the applicable ID/selfie or legacy original-ID process can be recorded as manually verified. The login help link goes to Contact rather than claiming to send a reset email.

The full service supports direct navigation and refresh on 15 routes: `/`, `/shop`, `/our-space`, `/about`, `/membership`, `/contact`, `/signup`, `/login`, `/account`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/responsible-use`, `/help`, `/staff`, and `/admin`. Unknown page paths show a page-not-found view. Account pages require member access; staff pages use their own authentication.

## Staff member directory and catalog

Only authenticated staff/admin sessions can read **`/api/admin/members`** or manage **`/api/admin/products`**. Member sessions do not grant this access. **`/staff` → Members** provides paginated search across registered accounts and verification statuses without displaying full birth dates or identity images in the directory. Identity images remain restricted to the review workflow. The administrator workspace includes Overview, Staff accounts and the four operational tabs. The staff workspace provides Orders, Members, Products and Age Checks. Only administrators can read `/api/admin/overview` or manage `/api/admin/staff`. Staff accounts are individual, always have the staff role, and cannot promote themselves. The administrator can create, reset, disable or re-enable them; disabling, password resets and username changes invalidate existing employee sessions. Keep credentials private and review access when an employee leaves.

**`/staff` → Products** supports draft creation, detail and size-price edits, publication and archive. Revision checks return a conflict when another session has changed a product. Changes and audit metadata persist in SQLite. The original collection seeds once; restarting does not replace staff changes or republish archived products. Only published entries reach **`/api/products`**, and the full Render storefront uses that catalog and current server prices for checkout. Publishing is a visibility action, not proof of actual stock, catalog approval or sales authority.

To add a photograph, select **Product photo** in the editor, choose a static JPEG, PNG or WebP of at most **6 MiB**, use **Upload photo**, then save the product. Source dimensions must be at least **64 × 64 pixels** and at most **20 megapixels**. The server decodes the source, strips metadata and stores a WebP limited to **1,600 pixels per side** in the same persistent SQLite database. Draft previews use authenticated staff endpoints; the public media endpoint returns the photo only while a published product references it. Unreferenced uploads expire after 24 hours, and the current store limits product media to 128 MiB. Saved draft, published and archived references protect their images from orphan cleanup. Public product media is separate from encrypted private identity evidence.

Pages and offline HTML remain bundled snapshots of the source collection. Staff product edits update the full Render site; rebuild/export and republish a snapshot to update those static copies. They do not receive member data or identity photographs.

## Account notifications and membership cards

The current number format is LC- followed by six numeric digits. Startup atomically converts earlier long-format numbers once, preserving old references as private lookup aliases. Current six-digit numbers remain stable. Issued references are reserved after account deletion without a remaining member link to prevent reuse. Back up the whole persistent SQLite database, including the alias table, with the existing backup process.

Both workspaces include **Search member ID** and **Scan member card**. Barcode images are decoded on the device; only the number is posted to the authenticated, rate-limited member-lookup endpoint. Camera access requires an explicit scanner action and a secure browser context. The service allows same-origin camera use so the sign-in document can route to the staff scanner; microphone and location remain disallowed. No camera is started on public pages.

Deploying the updated full service creates the private notification and membership-number tables in the same persistent SQLite database. Preserve the existing database path and mounted disk. No additional service, mail credential or browser push subscription is needed for the in-app notification feature.

Both authenticated workspaces expose a **Notifications** bell and activity view for new membership applications, submitted verification information, completed staff reviews and actual member-name changes. `/api/admin/notifications` requires workforce authentication; `/api/admin/notifications/:id/read` and `/api/admin/notifications/read-all` are protected POST actions. Read receipts are separate for each individual account and persist across restart. The legacy shared staff login shares one inbox. Mark-all actions use a displayed sequence boundary, leaving newer events unread.

The dashboard refreshes about every 15 seconds while visible and on focus/reconnection; it must be open for in-app updates. This does not enable email, operating-system push or sound alerts. There is no notification backfill for earlier account history. Event retention is up to 30 days and at most 10,000 records, and deleting a member cascades its events and read receipts. Event storage uses references rather than copied profile data; authenticated responses look up the member's current name. Full birth dates, identity photographs, email addresses and passwords are excluded from the feed. Include these tables in the existing restricted backup/retention process.

The membership-number migration backfills existing accounts atomically and gives new signups a random permanent `LC-123456` number. Profile changes and redeployments do not issue replacement numbers. The number is visible to its member and authorised staff and can be searched in the staff directory. The member dashboard's digital card and **Download membership card** SVG show the name, number, joining date and current verification state. Downloaded copies are private snapshots, not live status, government identification, proof of age or account approval. No new government-ID field or biometric processing is introduced.

For acceptance, use a local fixture with two workforce accounts: create a member, confirm both inboxes receive the event, read it as one account and confirm the other remains unread, then restart and check persistence. Exercise a synthetic verification submission and a name change, follow each record link and confirm member/public sessions cannot read the feed. Check the digital card on desktop/mobile, pending status, number stability and SVG contents without using a real person's ID or sending real mail.

## Welcome-email sender

Each successful new signup queues one welcome message in the same transaction as account creation. There is no historical-member backfill and no marketing subscription. The generic message confirms account creation and directs the member to their current verification status without passwords, birth dates, identity images or product promotions. An asynchronous worker processes due jobs with bounded retries; mail outages do not turn a completed signup into an error. Missing or invalid sender configuration leaves jobs pending rather than claiming they were sent. Queued jobs persist across restart and can be processed after the sender is configured.

Choose **SMTP** for a mailbox the owner controls, or **Resend** with a verified sender. Store the real values only in Render **Environment**. Set `EMAIL_PROVIDER` to `smtp` or `resend`, set `EMAIL_FROM` to the authorised sender address and optionally set `EMAIL_REPLY_TO`. `EMAIL_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` may override account links; otherwise the service uses `PUBLIC_ORIGIN` or Render's external URL. Links must use the genuine full-service HTTPS origin.

The requested public contact and reply address is **`loudpluscana@protonmail.com`**. Set `CONTACT_EMAIL` and `EMAIL_REPLY_TO` to it in the existing Render service. Proton's direct SMTP submission requires a paid Proton Mail plan and an active **custom-domain** sending address; the `@protonmail.com` contact address itself cannot use this service. With an eligible sender, generate a Proton SMTP token and use `SMTP_HOST=smtp.protonmail.ch`, `SMTP_PORT=587`, that token's custom-domain address for `SMTP_USER` and `EMAIL_FROM`, and the generated token for `SMTP_PASSWORD`. Keep the ordinary Proton account password out of Render. See [Proton's official requirements](https://proton.me/support/smtp-submission) and the full steps in [EMAIL_SETUP.md](EMAIL_SETUP.md). Without a custom domain, retain Proton for contact/replies and configure another authorised sender; do not claim the free mailbox is an automated SMTP sender.

For other SMTP providers, supply `SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT` (`465`, `587` or `2525`), `SMTP_USER` and `SMTP_PASSWORD`. Port 465 uses TLS; ports 587/2525 require STARTTLS. For Resend, supply `RESEND_API_KEY` and an authorised verified-domain sender. A testing sender restricted to the provider account's own address cannot welcome arbitrary new members. Render free services restrict common SMTP ports; use the actual host plan's supported transport or Resend's HTTPS API. [EMAIL_SETUP.md](EMAIL_SETUP.md) also covers an existing iCloud mailbox and its private app-specific password.

The Members view exposes welcome-email status: pending, sending, sent, failed or not queued. **Sent means provider acceptance, not guaranteed inbox delivery.** The administrator can retry a failed queued message after fixing the sender; it does not create a welcome job for historical members or resend an already accepted message. SMTP cannot promise exactly-once delivery if a process stops after remote acceptance but before local confirmation. Sender authentication, inbox placement, bounce handling and the business's email/privacy obligations remain operating duties. Member deletion removes unsent queued jobs, but does not recall an email already in flight or sent. Email verification and password-reset mail remain separate features.

## Operating notes

The database contains member names, emails, password hashes, saved favourites, session hashes, age/terms/upload consent records, temporary pending dates of birth, encrypted pending ID and selfie images, question identifiers/pass timestamps, verification status/audit metadata, persistent catalog records and customer order details. ID images may show document numbers; no separate ID-number field is extracted and no biometric template is created. Uploaded JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP images must each be at most 6 MiB, at least 200 × 200 pixels and at most 20 megapixels. The server decodes/re-encodes them to metadata-stripped JPEG, at most 2400 pixels on either side, and encrypts the image bytes with AES-256-GCM. Files are not served from a public upload directory.

Images are removed after approval, rejection, replacement/name invalidation, account deletion or at most seven days pending. The pending birth date has a maximum 30-day period and is removed after review or application expiry; cleanup runs on startup, periodically while the process runs, and during relevant operations. Historical backups and SQLite WAL copies need their own retention, deletion and restricted-access procedure. Live-database deletion is not a claim of immediate secure erasure from all storage. Loss or rotation of `IDENTITY_UPLOAD_KEY` makes older pending ciphertext unreadable, so use a deliberate key and image-lifecycle procedure rather than silently regenerating it. Restrict staff and host access, use HTTPS, maintain tested backups, and apply the business's retention policy. Changing `ADMIN_PASSWORD` requires secure administrator-secret handling and a redeployment. Reset individual employee passwords in Admin → Staff accounts and distribute each replacement securely. `STAFF_PASSWORD` is a legacy shared-login option, not the employee password store. Member passwords are set by members through the HTTPS account forms. Do not send passwords through chat or include them in the client ZIP.

Before collecting production identity-related data, the owner must supply the genuine responsible-party/contact details, document a lawful processing basis and retention schedule, verify operator agreements/security, review the actual cross-border hosting basis under POPIA section 72, and account for DOB and encrypted-image copies in backups and WAL files. These operational duties cannot be completed by changing an application flag; see [the legal and operating guide](SOUTH_AFRICAN_SIGNUP.md).

Check service logs for errors while keeping customer data and secrets out of shared logs. Redeploy code changes through the normal Git/Render process; the data disk should remain mounted at the same path.
